# Privacy

LemChat has no servers of its own and no analytics. Crash reports stay on your phone and are only shared if you copy them yourself.

The app connects directly to Twitch, 7TV, FrankerFaceZ, BetterTTV, dank.chat for DankChat badges, and GitHub for update checks. Depending on your settings and what you use, it also connects to the message history service, Supinic and the media upload service. Each of these services has its own privacy policy.
